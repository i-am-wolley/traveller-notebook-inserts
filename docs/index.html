    Printing it

    1. Print the imposed PDF on A4 at 100% / Actual size — never “Fit to page”.
    2. Double-sided, flipping on the short edge for regular and the long edge for passport. Flip edges vary by driver, so hold a sheet to the light: the back of page 1 must be page 2, the same way up.
    3. Trim the dashed cut lines, fold down the dashed centre, nest the sheets in order, and staple through the fold.

    Blank pages are added automatically: one to keep a two-page spread facing, and as many as it takes to reach a multiple of four, which is what a folded booklet needs.
