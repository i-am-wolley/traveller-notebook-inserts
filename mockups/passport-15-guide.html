Printing and folding

1PrintOpen year-book-passport-A4.pdf — the imposed file, not this one. A4, portrait, 100% / Actual size (never Fit to page), double-sided, flipping on the long edge. 2 sheets of paper come out.
2CheckHold a sheet up to the light. The back of page 1 must be page 2, the same way up. If it is upside down or the wrong page, your printer flipped on the other edge — change that setting and print again.
3CutTrim along the dashed cut lines. Two down the sides and four across, which also separates the two folded sheets on each piece of paper.
4FoldFold each sheet along the dashed centre line. Sheet one folds with pages 1 and 16 outside, sheet two with 3 and 14 outside, and so on inwards.
5NestLay each sheet inside the one before it (the top half of each A4 comes first). Thumb through: the numbers should run 1 to 16.
6BindTwo staples through the fold, or punch three holes on the fold for the notebook's elastic.
