Printing and folding

1PrintOpen sudoku-regular-A4.pdf — the imposed file, not this one. A4, landscape, 100% / Actual size (never Fit to page), double-sided, flipping on the short edge. 5 sheets of paper come out.
2CheckHold a sheet up to the light. The back of page 1 must be page 2, the same way up. If it is upside down or the wrong page, your printer flipped on the other edge — change that setting and print again.
3CutTrim along the dashed cut lines. Two of them, one down each side. Nothing to trim top or bottom — the insert is exactly A4 height.
4FoldFold each sheet along the dashed centre line. Sheet one folds with pages 1 and 20 outside, sheet two with 3 and 18 outside, and so on inwards.
5NestLay each sheet inside the one before it. Thumb through: the numbers should run 1 to 20.
6BindTwo staples through the fold, or punch three holes on the fold for the notebook's elastic.

What goes where

Each A4 side before cutting. The dashed line down the middle is the fold; the dashed lines at the sides are the cuts.
