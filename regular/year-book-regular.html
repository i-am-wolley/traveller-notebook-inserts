Printing and folding

1PrintOpen year-book-regular-A4.pdf — the imposed file, not this one. A4, landscape, 100% / Actual size (never Fit to page), double-sided, flipping on the short edge. 2 sheets of paper come out.
2CheckHold a sheet up to the light. The back of page 1 must be page 2, the same way up. If it is upside down or the wrong page, your printer flipped on the other edge — change that setting and print again.
3CutTrim along the dashed cut lines. Two of them, one down each side. Nothing to trim top or bottom — the insert is exactly A4 height.
4FoldFold each sheet along the dashed centre line. Sheet one folds with pages 1 and 8 outside, sheet two with 3 and 6 outside, and so on inwards.
5NestLay each sheet inside the one before it. Thumb through: the numbers should run 1 to 8.
6BindTwo staples through the fold, or punch three holes on the fold for the notebook's elastic.
